Chapter 02 — Vision & Mission

Born to raise, built to transform.

The vision that gave birth to this platform — and the mission and mandate that carry it forward.

The Vision

People ofexceptional value.

Our Vision

To raise people of great capacity

To raise people who have great capacity for problem solving — and become people of exceptional value and character.

Our Mission

Development & empowerment

Development and empowerment of men and women with skills, godly values — building a new world of development and greater achievement through transformation.

The Mandate

Twelve pillars.One assignment.

The mandate of the Multipurpose Talented: to serve as Transformers and Problem Solvers — solution finders — for the family, cities, villages, countries and nations.

01

Transformers & Problem Solvers

To serve as transformers and problem solvers — solution finders — for the family, cities, villages, countries and nations.

02

Educate, Imbue & Impact

To educate, imbue and impact individuals — building capacity wherever we find ourselves.

03

Freedom from Addiction

To set free people of all sorts of addiction — restoring lives and destinies.

04

Goal Setters & Achievers

To serve as goal setters — and goal achievers — showing what disciplined vision can accomplish.

05

The Seven Spheres

To bring total transformation in the seven spheres of influence — family, religion, education, media, arts, business and government.

06

Gatherers of Positive Minds

To gather people of positive mindset who will help bring forth total change in the society.

07

Root Out the Tares

To root out the tares among the wheat — guarding the purity of what we build.

08

Expose Every Evil

To expose every form of evil — standing as light in dark places.

09

Arms for the Needy

To give arms and provisions to the needy and the less privileged — generosity as a lifestyle.

10

Battle Axes

To serve as battle axes to root out the evil in the land.

11

New Development Strategies

To bring new development strategies and inventions — fresh thinking for old problems.

12

People of Exemplary Life

To raise people of exemplary life and of great standards — and to provide ideas, counsel and knowledge.
