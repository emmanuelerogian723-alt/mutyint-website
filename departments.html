Chapter 03 — The Departments

Five engines, one assignment.

The movement is organized into sections of departments — each one a working engine of the transformation mandate.

E

The core of the movement — where ideas are forged into work. The Engine Room conceives, develops and powers everything MUTYINT builds, turning raw talent into working systems.

For the builders and crafters of the movement.
G
Eggint

The strategy engine — turning vision into goals, and goals into achieved results. Goal Setters keep the movement disciplined, focused and always moving forward.

Society

The serving engine — carrying the transformation mandate to communities, cities and nations. From giving arms and provisions to the needy, to setting people free and bringing change to society.

The engine of the name itself — the researchers, creators and game changers. Inventors build what has never existed; transformers turn negative to positive and bring total development to every issue and problem.

For the inventors, researchers and transformers of the movement.
